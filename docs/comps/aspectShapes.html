What this is for

The world's shape is canvasAspect, a preference — it defines the shape of the simulated world, not a crop of the element (sizing.ts: world space is area-preserving, so the canvas keeps its pixel count and just gets wider and shorter). Changing it reallocates the entity buffer and both textures, and initialConditions.ts invalidates the captured scene when it does. That is why each shape needs its own initial conditions, and why switching shapes mid-authoring is destructive by design.

Read the fill figure: it is the fraction of the stage the canvas claims. Below roughly 70% the hatching is doing more work than the simulation, which is the argument for a third shape rather than letterboxing one.
